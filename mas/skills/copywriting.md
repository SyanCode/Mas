# Copywriting

## Rules
1. **Specific beats impressive.** Names, places, numbers, prices, delays, materials, steps. "Delivered in 48 h in
   Lyon" beats "Fast delivery".
2. **Facts come from the repository, the CMS content or the person.** Never invent a figure, a review, a client, a
   diploma, an address or a guarantee. Missing → `[TO FILL: …]` and say it in the report.
3. **One reader, one action.** Each page has a main visitor and one main action; everything else supports it.
4. **Write like the owner talks.** Keep the site's register (tu/vous, formal/casual) and language everywhere.

## Page structure
- Headline: what it is + for whom or where, in under ~10 words. Subheadline: the concrete benefit or proof.
- Then: what the visitor gets (specific), proof (real), how it works (steps), objections (price, delay, risk), action.
- Headings readable on their own: a visitor scanning only the h2s understands the page.

## Calls to action
- Verb + object, about what happens next: "Book a table", "Get the quote in 24 h", "See the menu". Not "Submit",
  "Learn more", "Get started" on everything.

## Microcopy
- Errors say what happened and how to fix it ("This email looks incomplete: check the part after @").
- Empty states say what goes there and offer the first action.
- Confirmations say what was done and what comes next.

## Banned (rewrite every occurrence)
unlock, unleash, elevate, empower, supercharge, revolutionize, transform your, seamless(ly), effortless(ly),
cutting-edge, state-of-the-art, game-changer, next level, world-class, best-in-class, robust, leverage, harness the
power, in today's fast-paced world, delve, embark, journey (for a purchase), tailored solutions, look no further,
"Welcome to our website". French equivalents too: « révolutionnez », « boostez », « sublimez », « de pointe »,
« solutions sur mesure » sans dire lesquelles, « n'attendez plus », « dans un monde en constante évolution ».

## Style
- Short paragraphs (2–4 lines), varied sentence length, active voice, no exclamation marks in series.
- No em-dash chains, no systematic triplets, no rhetorical questions as headings.
- Numbers as digits; units and currency in the local format (`12,50 €`, `9 h – 18 h`).
- French: espace insécable avant `: ; ! ?` et dans « », apostrophe typographique ’, majuscules accentuées (É, À).
